# How corporate customers pay

Two options at checkout:

- **Invoice** — the normal corporate route. The order is placed, an invoice is
  raised against the billing details on the order, and it is paid on terms. No
  card is needed to complete the order.
- **Card** — Visa, Mastercard and Amex, for teams who would rather not raise a PO.

## Purchase orders

A PO number is not needed to place the order and the agent must never ask for
one. It can be added to the account afterwards, and it appears on the invoice.

## What the buyer needs at checkout

First name, last name, work email, company name, and a billing address. Nothing
else. UK billing addresses do not need a county.

## Invoices afterwards

Invoices are listed in the account area and can be downloaded as PDFs. The
payment method shown on the subscription is the one the order was placed with.

## VAT

Prices are quoted excluding VAT, which is applied at checkout according to the
billing address.
