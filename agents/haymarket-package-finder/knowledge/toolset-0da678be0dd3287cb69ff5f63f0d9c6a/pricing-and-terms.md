# Prices and terms

| Package | Price | Billed |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign Corporate | £249 per seat per year | annually |
| Horticulture Week Corporate | £293 per seat per year | annually |
| The Healthcare Package | £288 per seat per year | annually |
| All-Access Corporate | £295 per seat per year | annually |
| All-Access Corporate — Monthly | £32 per seat per month | monthly |

## Annual against monthly

Monthly at £32 a seat is **£384 per seat over a year**. Annual All-Access is
**£295**. Annual saves **£89 per seat — 23%**.

Worked examples for All-Access:

| Seats | Annual | Monthly over a year | Saved by going annual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | £1,475 | £1,920 | £445 |
| 10 | £2,950 | £3,840 | £890 |
| 12 | £3,540 | £4,608 | £1,068 |
| 25 | £7,375 | £9,600 | £2,225 |
| 50 | £14,750 | £19,200 | £4,450 |

Annual is the recommendation unless the visitor says they want to start short,
cannot commit to a year, or are trialling.

## Term

Annual licences run for a year from the day the order is placed and renew
automatically. Monthly runs month to month.

## Seats

Between 1 and 250 seats, bought in single seats. There is no minimum and no seat
band — twelve seats costs twelve times the seat price. Seats can be added or
removed from the account; changes are prorated against the remaining term.

Above 250 seats it stops being self-serve — see the large and bespoke document.
