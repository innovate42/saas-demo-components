# Bulk print copies — £95 per copy per year

Printed copies of the titles in the licence, delivered to the office each issue.

- Priced **per copy per year**, on top of the digital seats
- Its own quantity, independent of the seat count — ten digital seats and five
  printed copies for the staff room is a normal shape
- Added to the same order and the same invoice as the licence
- Delivered to the billing address on the order

Print is an addition to a licence, not a replacement for it. There is no
print-only corporate subscription.

Offer it once, after the licence is settled. If the visitor says no, leave it.
