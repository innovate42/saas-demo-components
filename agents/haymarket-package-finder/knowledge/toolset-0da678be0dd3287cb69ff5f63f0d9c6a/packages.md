# The five corporate packages

Every package is a licence with one seat per named person. A seat is an
individual login; seats are not shared.

## Campaign Corporate — £249 per seat per year
For marketing, advertising, PR and media agencies.
- Campaign UK, US, Asia and Canada in one licence
- Campaign's Annual Salary Survey
- Global agency rankings and the bi-annual M&A round-up
- School Reports and the Power 100
- **PRWeek is included** — name it to a PR agency

## Horticulture Week Corporate — £293 per seat per year
For growers, nurseries, landscaping firms and garden retail.
- Full HortWeek access for every named seat
- Daily and Weekly Bulletins
- Full Plant Library, more than 20 years of expert insight
- Pest and disease alerts and factsheets
- HortWeek In Depth Reports

Replaces the old fixed "up to 3-user" package — buy exactly the seats you need.

## The Healthcare Package — £288 per seat per year
For GP practices, pharmacy and clinical teams. Four titles in one licence:
- MIMS — trusted drug database, updated daily, 50+ drug reference tables
- MIMS Learning — 550+ CPD hours, easy appraisal integration
- GPonline — daily news, clinical education, careers
- GP Business — practice management tools, policy updates, the GP Fees Database
- The reports series for healthcare professionals is included

Replaces the old fixed "up to six users" package.

## All-Access Corporate — £295 per seat per year
Exactly five titles: Campaign, PRWeek, Horticulture Week, Windpower Monthly and
The ENDS Report.

**It does not include The Healthcare Package.** MIMS, MIMS Learning, GPonline
and GP Business are a separate licence. A business needing both buys both.

This is the right answer when a business spans two or more subject areas, when it
cannot be placed in a single sector, or when it wants energy and environment
coverage (Windpower Monthly and The ENDS Report are only sold inside All-Access).

At £295 it is cheaper than any two single-title packages together.

## All-Access Corporate — Monthly — £32 per seat per month
The same content, billed monthly, for teams that want to start short. See the
pricing document for what that costs over a year.
