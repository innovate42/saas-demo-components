# Choosing the plan

Four facts decide almost everything, in this order:

1. **ERP or no ERP.** Do orders and invoices live in an ERP or accounting system (NetSuite, SAP, Dynamics, QuickBooks, Sage...)? Or are they keyed by hand in a portal or spreadsheet?
   - No ERP: **B2B Integration Essentials**. The web portal turns a retailer's EDI into screens and forms.
   - ERP: **Trading Grid Foundation**. Documents flow straight in and out of the ERP, no re-keying.
2. **How many trading partners ask them for EDI** (retailers, distributors, manufacturers).
   - Essentials: 1-2 partners = Starter; 3-5 = Plus.
   - Trading Grid: up to 5 = Foundation 5; 6-10 = Foundation 5 or 10 (see add-ons for the cheaper mix); up to 15 with extra-partner add-ons.
   - More than 15: **Enterprise**.
   - No ERP and 6 to 8 partners: Essentials Plus with extra-partner add-ons, as long as the volume fits 5,000 documents a month. Above that volume, or above 8 partners, keying by hand stops scaling: recommend Trading Grid Foundation (it also takes an SFTP file drop exported from spreadsheets or an accounting package, no ERP adapter needed), and say why.
3. **Document volume per month** (purchase orders, ASNs and invoices together). Starter 1,000; Plus 5,000; Foundation 5 10,000; Foundation 10 25,000. Above 25,000: Enterprise. Each order produces about 3 documents (PO, ASN, invoice), so **documents per month = orders per month x 3**. Example: 2,000 orders a month = about 6,000 documents, which is above Essentials Plus. If they do not know, do not block on it.
4. **Who sets it up.** Self-service is included. "OpenText should do it" = Guided Onboarding add-on. "Our IT team with custom requirements", managed services, several ERPs or business units: Enterprise.

Region (see the regions document) only changes currency and data centre, never the plan.

Enterprise triggers, any one is enough: more than 15 partners, more than 25,000 documents a month, custom integration or managed services, several ERPs or legal entities, a procurement or security review before buying.
