# Add-ons

| Add-on | What it does | USD | GBP | EUR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ERP Adapter | Pre-built connector for the customer's ERP on Trading Grid Foundation (NetSuite, SAP Business One, SAP S/4HANA, Dynamics 365 Business Central and F&O, QuickBooks Online, Sage Intacct, Acumatica, Epicor Kinetic, Infor CloudSuite). Other ERPs connect by SFTP file drop or API with no adapter. | $149 / month | £119 | €139 |
| Extra Trading Partner | One more trading partner on top of what a Trading Grid Foundation plan includes | $79 / month each | £65 | €75 |
| Premium Support | 24x7 support with a faster response target | $199 / month | £159 | €185 |
| Guided Onboarding | One-time. A named OpenText B2B specialist runs the setup with the customer: kickoff call, ERP and connectivity setup, partner testing, go-live. | $1,500 once | £1,200 | €1,400 |

Rules of thumb:
- When the partner count is just above a plan, compare a bigger plan with extra-partner add-ons and suggest the cheaper. Example: 7 partners on Trading Grid = Foundation 5 + 2 extra partners ($499 + $158 = $657) is cheaper than Foundation 10 ($899); 9 or 10 partners = Foundation 10.
- Extra partners top out at 15 partners in total. Above 15, it is Enterprise.
- "We want OpenText to set it up" = Guided Onboarding. It is the honest answer to that wish on any plan. Without it the customer follows a self-service checklist, and most still go live within about 9 days.
- The Trading Grid page adds the ERP adapter automatically when the customer picks an ERP that has one.
