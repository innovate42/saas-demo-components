# Regions

| Region | Currency | Data centre |
|---|---|---|
| Americas (US, Canada, Latin America) | USD | North America |
| United Kingdom | GBP | EMEA (UK) |
| Europe | EUR | EMEA (EU) |
| Asia-Pacific | USD | Asia-Pacific |

- The store shows prices in the customer's region; the region picker sits in the page header.
- The Trading Grid tenant is created in the data centre of their region; data stays there.
- Partners anywhere in the world can be connected whatever the region (UK grocers use Tradacoms or EDIFACT, EU retailers EDIFACT, US retailers X12). OpenText loads the partner maps.
